## 2. Staff Workflows by Role

### 2.1 Cashier (कॅशियर)
* **Primary Duty**: Billing, POS checkout, cash/UPI receipt, and collecting farmer Khata repayments.
* **Prohibitions**: Cannot view shop profit, purchase wholesale margins, or adjust inventory.

### 2.2 Agronomist (कृषी तज्ञ / सल्लागार)
* **Primary Duty**: Assisting farmers with crop protection schedules, diagnosing pests/fungi, and building quotations.
* **Prohibitions**: Cannot finalize financial POS sales or enter purchase orders.

### 2.3 Manager (व्यवस्थापक)
* **Primary Duty**: Inward stock receipt, batch verification, physical count audits, and managing product availability.
* **Prohibitions**: Cannot view confidential owner net profit or modify staff credentials.

### 2.4 Accountant (हिशोबनीस)
* **Primary Duty**: Operating expense entry, supplier invoice reconciliation, GST tax ledger verification, and P&L audit.
* **Prohibitions**: Cannot modify system settings or access private Owner AI.
