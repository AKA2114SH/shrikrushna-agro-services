# Admin User Manual & System Management Guide
### Shri Krishna Agro Services — प्रणाली व्यवस्थापन मॅन्युअल

---

## 1. ERP Shell Navigation & Modules
The Admin ERP shell provides a consolidated workspace for business operations:

1. **Dashboard (डॅशबोर्ड)**: Real-time sales, live net profit, customer debt, supplier payables, and reorder warnings.
2. **Sales POS (POS विक्री)**: High-speed billing with barcode / search lookup, GST calculation, split payment modes, and instant A4 invoice printing.
3. **Products (उत्पादने)**: Product catalog management, pack sizes, retail selling prices, and technical agrochemical specs.
4. **Inventory (साठा / इन्व्हेंटरी)**: Current stock levels, batch numbers, manufacturing/expiry dates, and stock adjustment tools.
5. **Purchases (खरेदी)**: Inward supplier delivery logs, landed cost calculation, and supplier payment history.
6. **Customers (शेतकरी ग्राहक)**: Farmer CRM directory, acreage data, crop records, and Khata debt repayment ledger.
7. **Suppliers (सप्लायर)**: Manufacturer directories, GSTIN records, invoice histories, and payment disbursements.
8. **Quotations (दरपत्रक)**: Price quote creation, status management, and 1-click conversion to finalized sales.
9. **Expenses (दुकान खर्च)**: Operating overhead logging across 9 categories.
10. **Reports (हिशोब व अहवाल)**: Comprehensive P&L statement, GST sales reports, and stock movement ledgers.
11. **WhatsApp (व्हॉट्सॲप)**: Bidirectional message logs, quotation sharing, and Khata debt reminders.
12. **Owner AI (बिझनेस एआय)**: Conversational business intelligence query assistant.
13. **Settings (सेटिंग्ज)**: Business legal profile, statutory licenses, staff account governance, and offline backup/restore.

## 2. Staff Account & Role Governance
* To add or deactivate staff accounts, open **'सेटिंग्ज (Settings)'** $\to$ **'कर्मचारी व्यवस्थापन (Staff Governance)'**.
* Assign exact roles (`MANAGER`, `ACCOUNTANT`, `CASHIER`, `AGRONOMIST`) according to organizational duties.
* System enforces a maximum ceiling of 2 Owner accounts (Proprietors Shubham Gamane and Jagdish Bodke).
